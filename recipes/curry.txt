Spicy Mango Chickpea Curry

Ingredients:
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (optional, for extra heat)
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 1 can (14 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 medium sweet potato, peeled and diced
- 1 cup fresh spinach leaves
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
- Cooked basmati rice or naan bread (for serving)

Instructions:
1. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and sauté until soft and golden, about 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the garlic and ginger, cooking for another minute until fragrant.
3. Add the cumin, coriander, turmeric, paprika, and cayenne pepper. Stir well to coat the onions in the spices.
4. Pour in the diced tomatoes and cook for 5 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together.
5. Add the coconut milk, chickpeas, and sweet potato. Stir to combine and bring to a gentle simmer.
6. Cover the pan and cook for 15-20 minutes, or until the sweet potato is tender.
7. Stir in the fresh spinach leaves and cook for another 2-3 minutes until wilted.
8. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
9. Serve hot over basmati rice or with naan bread, garnished with fresh cilantro.

Enjoy your homemade Spicy Coconut Chickpea Curry!
